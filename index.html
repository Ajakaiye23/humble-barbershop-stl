06 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Do you take walk-ins?
No — the shop is appointment only. Book through Booksy and the chair is yours when you arrive, with no waiting room and no queue.
Where exactly are you?
425 Clark St, St. Charles. The shop is inside the Gimme Golf Clubhouse, which is worth knowing before you go looking for a shopfront.
What does a cut cost?
A haircut is $45 and takes about 50 minutes. Haircut and beard together is $60. Beard trim and line up is $30, a line up on its own is $25, and eyebrows or nose/ear wax are $10 each.
Can I bring my kids, or my dog?
Both. The shop is child-friendly and pet-friendly, and there is free Wi-Fi while you wait.
How do I book?
Through Booksy, which carries the live calendar, or send a request from this page and we will confirm a slot.
